Explain **$ARGUMENTS** to someone who is learning data engineering and wants to understand the why, not just the what. Check `guide/glossary.md` in case there is already a definition.

Structure (brief, in the learner's language):
1. **What it is**, in one sentence and without jargon.
2. **An everyday analogy**.
3. **Why it matters**: what breaks or how much it costs if ignored, with a concrete example from this project (pumps, sensors, bronze/silver/gold…).
4. **What it looks like in a real job** (tools or practices where they will meet it).
5. **A question** to check they understood. Do not give the answer: wait.
